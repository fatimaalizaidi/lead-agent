# Enterprise Onboarding

Typical Enterprise onboarding takes **2–4 weeks** from contract signature to production cutover.

## Week 0 — Kickoff
- Assign a Customer Success Manager (CSM) and Solutions Engineer (SE)
- Confirm SSO IdP, data residency, and retention requirements
- Share technical contacts and security questionnaire deadlines

## Weeks 1–2 — Configuration
- Provision tenant and SSO (SAML or OIDC)
- Connect CRM / webhook destinations (Salesforce, HubSpot, or custom HTTPS)
- Import historical leads (CSV or API); optional backfill up to 90 days
- Configure scoring rules and priority thresholds with the SE

## Weeks 3–4 — Validation & go-live
- Run a pilot with 5–10 internal users
- Review sample analyses against your playbook
- Enable production traffic and hand off runbooks to your ops team

Professional services for custom integrations or data migration are quoted separately. A dedicated Slack or Teams channel is opened for Enterprise accounts during onboarding.
