# Product Features

ReplyHub is a demo company (fictional) that sells WhatsApp team-inbox software. A business connects one WhatsApp number, and its whole team answers customers from a shared inbox. Each person who logs in to answer chats uses one **agent seat**.

## Shared inbox
- One WhatsApp Business number, many agents answering at the same time
- Every chat shows who is handling it, so two agents never reply to the same customer
- Internal notes on a chat are visible to the team only, never to the customer

## Auto-assign
- New chats are assigned automatically by **round-robin** or to the **least busy** agent
- Rules can route chats by keyword or language, for example "refund" to the billing team
- Auto-assign is included on Growth and Enterprise; Starter assigns chats manually

## Chat tags and quick replies
- Tag chats (for example "order", "complaint", "lead") and filter the inbox by tag
- Saved quick replies that every agent can use, with placeholders for the customer's name

## Reports
- First-response time, chats per agent and open chats by tag
- Reports are on Growth and Enterprise; Enterprise can export them to CSV

## Instagram DMs (optional add-on)
- Answer Instagram Direct Messages in the same inbox as WhatsApp
- Available as an add-on on Growth and Enterprise for **$10 per agent seat per month**
- Not available on Starter

## Integrations
- Webhooks for new chats and tags; connect to Shopify, a CRM or a Google Sheet
- A REST API is included on Enterprise
