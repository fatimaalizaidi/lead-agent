# Pricing Plans

Acme LeadDesk offers three public plans. All prices are in USD and billed monthly; annual billing receives a 15% discount.

## Starter — $49 / month
- Up to 3 seats
- 1,000 analyzed leads per month
- Email + Slack notifications
- Standard email support (business hours)

## Growth — $149 / month
- Up to 15 seats
- 10,000 analyzed leads per month
- Priority scoring, custom tags, CRM webhooks
- Chat support with 4-hour first-response target on business days

## Enterprise — custom quote
- Unlimited seats (fair-use)
- Unlimited analyzed leads
- SSO (SAML/OIDC), dedicated VPC option, custom retention
- Named Customer Success Manager and 24×7 incident channel

Volume discounts start at 50 seats on Growth. Public list prices do not include professional services or custom integrations; those are scoped separately.
