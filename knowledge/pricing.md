# Pricing Plans

ReplyHub (a demo company) has three plans. Prices are **per agent seat per month** in USD. An agent seat is one person who logs in to answer chats. Annual billing receives a **15% discount**.

**Which plan fits your team size:** 1–5 people answering WhatsApp → Starter; 6–25 people → Growth; 26 or more people → Enterprise (custom quote from $49 per agent seat).

## Starter plan: $15 per agent seat / month
- 1 to 5 agent seats
- 1 WhatsApp number
- Shared inbox, chat tags and quick replies
- Manual chat assignment (no auto-assign)
- Email support during business hours

## Growth plan: $29 per agent seat / month
- Up to 25 agent seats
- Up to 3 WhatsApp numbers
- Auto-assign (round-robin or least busy), reports and webhooks
- Instagram DMs available as a $10 per seat add-on
- Chat support with a 4-hour first-response target on business days

## Enterprise plan: custom quote, from $49 per agent seat / month
- 26 or more agent seats (no upper limit)
- Unlimited WhatsApp numbers
- REST API, SSO (SAML), CSV report export, custom data retention
- Named Customer Success Manager and a 24×7 incident channel

## Volume discounts and extra costs
- Volume discounts start at **50 agent seats** (Enterprise).
- WhatsApp conversation fees charged by Meta are **not included** in ReplyHub prices; they are billed by Meta through your WhatsApp Business account.
- Free 14-day trial on Starter and Growth, no credit card needed.
