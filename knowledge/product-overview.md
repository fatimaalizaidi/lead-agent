# Product Overview: What Acme LeadDesk Does

Acme LeadDesk is an AI inbox for inbound sales messages. Businesses connect their website forms, contact email and chat to it. Every new message is read by AI within seconds, given a priority, and handed to the right person with a suggested reply, so ready-to-buy customers are answered first and are never buried under routine questions.

## The problem it solves
Small teams answer everything in arrival order. When one person handles new enquiries, existing-customer questions and scheduling together, new leads wait hours or days and go to a competitor. LeadDesk separates and ranks the incoming messages so the team sees the most valuable ones first.

## How it works
1. **Capture:** messages arrive from web forms, a shared inbox (email forwarding) or chat. CRM webhooks and the API cover other sources.
2. **Score:** AI reads each message and assigns **High** (ready to buy: budget, timeline, urgency), **Medium** (interested, comparing or asking questions) or **Low** (spam, vague or not a fit).
3. **Draft:** it suggests a reply grounded in your own pricing and policy documents, for your team to send or edit.
4. **Route:** High leads alert the team at once in Slack or by message; Medium leads go to a follow-up queue; Low leads get a polite automatic reply or are filtered out.

## Who it is for
Any business that receives more enquiries than its team can answer quickly: software companies, agencies, clinics and other appointment-based services, education providers, real estate and e-commerce stores. It works for a team of one person as well as a large sales team.

**Example, a clinic:** a front-desk agent answers new patient enquiries, booking requests and existing patients' questions in one inbox. LeadDesk marks "I want to book a treatment package" as High and alerts the agent at once, groups appointment and rescheduling questions as Medium, and filters spam. New customers stop being overlooked while existing customers are still answered.

## Integrations
Inbox forwarding, Slack alerts, CRM webhooks to Salesforce or HubSpot, custom HTTPS webhooks and a REST API. There is no Microsoft Teams alert integration yet; other tools connect through webhooks or the API.

**Custom setup:** LeadDesk can be connected to WhatsApp Business (through the WhatsApp Business API) or to a business's own booking or CRM software (through webhooks or the API), so the messages customers already send are triaged where they arrive. Replies can be customized with the business's own packages, prices, timings and policies. Custom setups are scoped by the sales team.

## Billing changes
Seats can be added or removed at any time within a plan's seat limit; the change applies from the next billing cycle. Moving from Starter to Growth takes effect immediately.

## Accuracy and data
Accuracy depends on the business's own messages, so Acme does not publish a single accuracy figure. Before going live, sample analyses can be reviewed against the customer's own playbook. Acme does not publish customer names or industry case studies, and does not offer healthcare-specific (HIPAA) agreements; compliance questions go to the sales team.
