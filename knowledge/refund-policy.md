# Refund Policy

ReplyHub is a demo company; this policy is fictional.

## Monthly subscriptions
- Cancel anytime from the billing page; access continues until the end of the paid month
- **No prorated refunds** for unused days on monthly plans after the first 14 days of a new subscription

## Annual subscriptions
- Within **14 days** of purchase or renewal: full refund of the unused annual term
- After 14 days: no cash refund; the remaining months can be converted to account credit for a plan change (Starter, Growth or Enterprise)

## Removing agent seats
- Seats removed in the middle of a billing period are credited on the next invoice, not refunded in cash

## Enterprise contracts
- Governed by the signed Order Form
- Early termination fees, if any, are listed in the Order Form

## WhatsApp fees
- Conversation fees charged by Meta are paid to Meta and are never refunded by ReplyHub

Billing disputes go to billing support first (`billing@replyhub.example`). Chargebacks opened without contacting billing support may lead to account suspension.
