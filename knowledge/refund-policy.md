# Refund Policy

## Monthly subscriptions
- Cancel anytime from the billing portal; access continues through the end of the paid period
- **No prorated refunds** for unused days on monthly plans after the first 14 days of a new subscription

## Annual subscriptions
- Within **14 days** of initial purchase or renewal: full refund of the unused annual term if usage is under 5% of the plan's lead quota
- After 14 days: no cash refund; remaining months may be converted to account credit for plan changes (Growth ↔ Enterprise) at Acme's discretion

## Enterprise contracts
- Governed by the signed Order Form / MSA
- Early termination fees, if any, are listed in the Order Form
- Unused professional-services hours expire 12 months after purchase unless otherwise stated

Chargebacks should be disputed through billing support first (`billing@acmeleaddesk.example`). Fraudulent or abusive chargebacks may result in account suspension.
