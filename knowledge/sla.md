# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

ReplyHub is a demo company; this SLA is fictional. It applies to Growth and Enterprise customers. Starter is best-effort only.

## Availability
- Target uptime: **99.9%** monthly for the inbox, the mobile app and the API
- Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 72 hours in advance and happens on Sundays 02:00–05:00 PKT (Saturday 21:00–24:00 UTC) when possible
- Outages on Meta's side (WhatsApp Cloud API) are outside ReplyHub's SLA, but ReplyHub posts updates about them

## Response times (support tickets)
| Severity | Example | First response | Target resolution |
|----------|---------|----------------|-------------------|
| Sev-1 | Inbox down, or messages not being delivered | 30 minutes | 4 hours |
| Sev-2 | Auto-assign or reports broken, limited workaround | 2 hours | 1 business day |
| Sev-3 | Minor bug or question | 1 business day | 5 business days |

## Service credits
If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, Growth and Enterprise customers may request a credit of 10% of that month's fee for each full 0.1% below target, capped at 50% of the monthly fee. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the month.
