# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

This SLA applies to Growth and Enterprise customers on current contracts. Starter is best-effort only.

## Availability
- Target uptime: **99.9%** monthly for the production API and dashboard
- Scheduled maintenance windows are announced at least 72 hours in advance and occur Sundays 02:00–06:00 UTC when possible

## Response times (support tickets)
| Severity | First response | Target resolution |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| Sev-1 (production down / data loss risk) | 30 minutes | 4 hours |
| Sev-2 (major feature broken, workaround limited) | 2 hours | 1 business day |
| Sev-3 (degraded or non-critical) | 1 business day | 5 business days |

## Credits
If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, Growth and Enterprise customers may request a service credit of 10% of that month's subscription fee per full 0.1% below target, capped at 50% of the monthly fee. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident month.
