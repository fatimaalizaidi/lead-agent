# Support Hours

ReplyHub is a demo company; these hours are fictional.

## Standard support (Starter and Growth)
- **Monday to Saturday, 10:00–19:00 PKT** (Pakistan time, UTC+5)
- Channels: email (`support@replyhub.example`) and WhatsApp chat with the ReplyHub support team
- Growth customers get a 4-hour first-response target on business days
- Starter customers get email support only, with a next-business-day response

## Enterprise support
- 24×7 incident channel for Sev-1 and Sev-2 issues
- Named Customer Success Manager during business hours for non-urgent requests

## Public holidays
Support is closed on Eid ul-Fitr, Eid ul-Adha and other Pakistani public holidays for non-urgent tickets. Sev-1 incidents for Enterprise customers are still covered 24×7.

Status and incident updates are posted at `status.replyhub.example`.
