# Support Hours

## Standard support (Starter & Growth)
- **Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00** in the account's primary region (US Eastern or EU Central)
- Channels: email (`support@acmeleaddesk.example`) and in-app chat
- Growth customers receive a 4-hour first-response target on business days for chat and email

## Enterprise support
- 24×7 incident channel for Sev-1 / Sev-2 issues
- Named CSM available during business hours for non-urgent requests
- Optional follow-the-sun coverage for global deployments (contract add-on)

## Holidays
US and EU public holidays observed by the primary region may delay non-urgent tickets. Sev-1 Enterprise incidents remain covered 24×7 year-round.

Status and incident updates are posted at `status.acmeleaddesk.example`.
