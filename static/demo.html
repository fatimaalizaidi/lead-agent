An AI that reads customer messages and tells a sales team who to answer first.

Imagine you sell software and dozens of messages arrive every day. Some are ready to buy, some are just curious, and some are spam. This AI reads each message, decides how important it is, and writes a reply using the company's own pricing and policy documents.

The pretend company: Acme LeadDesk

Acme LeadDesk sells an online tool to sales teams. The tool sorts the messages a business receives from potential customers, so the team knows who to call first. Businesses pay a monthly subscription for each team:

Starter · $49/monthUp to 3 users, 1,000 messages a month
Growth · $149/monthUp to 15 users, 10,000 messages a month
Enterprise · custom priceUnlimited users, SSO, a dedicated manager

You play a business that might buy it. Ask about prices, discounts, refunds, uptime, support hours or setup time, say you're ready to buy, or send spam. The AI answers from Acme's documents.

What happens when you click

What the production API does with every lead the n8n workflow sends it. On this page GPT always runs as well, so you can compare the two models.

1 · Guardrails

Input checks

Length limits and prompt-injection detection. Try the 🛡️ example.

2 · Route

LoRA classifier first

A confident Low (spam, no buying intent) is answered here in ms, for free. GPT is never called.

3 · Retrieve

Hybrid RAG

Everything else: vector search + BM25 keyword search, fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion.

4 · Reason

GPT-4o-mini

Strict JSON schema output: priority, reply, rationale, sources.

5 · Remember

Redis memory

Follow-ups in the same thread see the earlier turns.
